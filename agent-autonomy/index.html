How AgentControl works

Hermes turns your brief into a coding prompt. OpenCode executes it. AgentControl collects file changes and runs your verification command, then Hermes decides the next turn.

You review permissions and answer questions. Steering notes enter the next planning turn. Pause waits at a turn boundary; Stop asks the active session to abort.

Goal checks are saved requirements. They remain pending until the backend can provide individual evidence. A passing verification command only proves what that command checks.

Shortcuts: a review approval · d deny and redirect · p pause/resume · x stop confirmation · / focus steering. Shortcuts are ignored while typing.
